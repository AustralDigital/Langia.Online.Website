# Langia Marketing 2026 image prompt set

## Generation mode

All bitmap assets were generated with Codex's built-in `imagegen` capability in prompt-driven generation mode. The two mobile companions were generated in reference-guided mode from their matching desktop campaign images so the people, wardrobe, and environments stay coherent. The editorial avatar is a deterministic, code-native SVG and was not generated as a bitmap.

## Shared art direction

Apply this direction to every scene:

> Ultra-realistic premium editorial photography for a global language-learning brand. Bright natural daylight, white or pale contemporary architecture, soft mist-blue atmosphere, controlled cobalt or Signal Blue details, and at most one restrained warm-gold accent. Show the life outcome of language confidence rather than classroom mechanics. Natural skin texture, believable hands, candid body language, calm confidence, generous air, refined photographic depth, and a coherent international campaign sensibility.

Shared negative constraints:

> No logos, brand names, readable text, flags, watermarks, fake UI, dark boardrooms, dark full-frame backgrounds, dramatic low-key lighting, handshake clichés, stock-photo posing, direct-to-camera grins, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, floating objects, robots, holograms, generic AI symbols, or embedded headlines.

## Asset prompts

1. `home/hero-professional-desktop.webp` — Landscape campaign anchor. A confident Latin American professional woman in a cobalt jacket arriving at a bright international conference environment, speaking naturally while walking. Place her in the right 45% with clean pale architecture and meaningful negative space on the left; preserve face, hands, bag, and stride.

2. `home/hero-professional-mobile.webp` — Reference the desktop hero's woman, cobalt jacket, wardrobe, and conference environment, but compose an independent vertical portrait. Keep her in the middle-right and lower-right with airy architecture above and to the left; do not simulate a crop from the landscape frame.

3. `programs/langia-online-global-presentation.webp` — A professional confidently presenting an idea to a small international team in a bright contemporary studio. Keep presenter and listeners in the central 55%, show believable eye contact and hands, and use only abstract, unreadable presentation material.

4. `corporate/global-team-presentation.webp` — A diverse international leadership team aligning on a cross-border initiative around an unbranded architectural model in a daylight executive studio. Keep faces and hands in the central 60%; the scene should feel strategic and collaborative, never like a staged boardroom handshake.

5. `shared/about-human-led-design.webp` — Two independent education professionals thoughtfully mapping a learner journey with blank planning cards in a calm, bright design studio. Present them as contextual models, not Langia employees; cards must contain no text or logos.

6. `shared/independent-educator-remote-work.webp` — An independent educator preparing for a warm live remote conversation in a bright home studio. Use a natural three-quarter moment, believable laptop placement, soft daylight, and a screen containing only abstract unbranded shapes. Do not imply the person is a named Langia employee.

7. `editorial/ai-assisted-human-outcome.webp` — A learner rehearsing an important presentation with a supportive human educator in a bright studio; a tablet with subtle abstract shapes is visibly secondary. Communicate human-led confidence and thoughtful technology without robots, holograms, dashboards, or AI iconography.

8. `home/family-travel-independence.webp` — A family communicating confidently as they arrive at a bright, unbranded international destination. Capture a candid exchange with a hospitality professional, natural family connection, and independent movement; avoid passports, phones, flags, signs, and tourist posing.

9. `programs/talkin-club-natural-connection.webp` — Two internationally minded professionals in a natural, lively conversation during a bright terrace gathering. Capture spontaneous eye contact and relaxed gestures with soft greenery and daylight; no devices, handshakes, flags, logos, or direct-camera pose.

10. `programs/test-prep-campus-readiness.webp` — A learner arriving calmly and confidently at a bright international campus, ready for the next academic milestone. Focus on forward movement and readiness rather than studying; no exam sheets, books, anxiety, graduation regalia, or readable institutional signs.

11. `programs/kids-family-discovery-desktop.webp` — A family exploring an original, non-branded cultural and amusement destination in bright daylight while a teenager confidently asks an attendant for guidance. Keep the family in the central 58%; make the place joyful and premium without recognizable park identities, mascots, school props, tablets, or cartoon styling.

12. `programs/kids-family-discovery-mobile.webp` — Reference the desktop family's people, wardrobe, and original destination, then build an independent vertical composition. Group the family in the central and lower frame with open sky and attraction depth above; show the teenager communicating naturally.

13. `editorial/choose-english-program-destinations.webp` — An elegant traveler pausing at a bright architectural junction whose distinct paths suggest professional and international possibilities. Create a visual metaphor for choosing a route, with the person in the central 50%; no comparison charts, readable wayfinding, flags, or passports.

14. `editorial/language-exam-next-step.webp` — A young adult speaking calmly and confidently in a bright university admissions atrium, suggesting a successful next step after exam preparation. Place the learner in the central-right 50% with a clean pale field on the left; no papers, books, anxious expression, caps, logos, or readable signage.

## Output handling

The accepted generations were visually reviewed at native resolution for brightness, anatomy, crop safety, embedded text, accidental logos, and campaign consistency. Runtime copies were converted to WebP at quality 88 with high compression effort. The exact source dimensions and placement notes are recorded in the image manifest.
