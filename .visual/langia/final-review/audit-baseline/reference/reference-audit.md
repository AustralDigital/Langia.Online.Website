## What translates well

- **0–7.5s: stable image, moving foreground.** Main headline and actions depart first, trust strip and case-study card follow, while a single flower visual anchors the composition. The image continuity makes the changing foreground legible. Langia can preserve this with a signature human photograph; independent language/photo changes compete for the same attention.
- **8–19s: distinct handoff.** The photo expands to full bleed, becomes defocused/darker and scales, then a white canvas takes over. Preserve the ordered state progression and test its scroll distance locally; recording duration alone cannot distinguish scroll travel from pauses.
- **19–23.5s: one transformation argument.** A headline introduces the before/after scene, then two cards express the change. The movement reveals the second state. Langia should consolidate its repeated transformation claim into one scene with distinct supporting facts.
- **24–31s: programs/services as editorial rows.** Wide image/text pairs share alignment, offer list, and one full-width action. The next row overlaps the departing one. Content changes while the composition remains familiar. Preserve Langia’s existing Programs concept; refine spacing, crop, and CTA hierarchy.
- **31–42s: small process interaction followed by media and quiet support copy.** At 37–39s cursor movement visibly corresponds to expanded panels 04→03→02→01 while the page remains nearly stationary. This is evidence of pointer-driven interaction. It does not justify arbitrary autoplay or a long scroll lock. The supporting media and support list have different jobs.
- **43–48.5s: team scene uses explicit people and roles.** The colored panel organizes actual staff rather than offering another abstract promise.
- **49–54s: credibility has separate evidence types.** Testimonial photo and named quotation; several additional quotations; compact metrics; one case study with outcome details. Langia should only allocate this many sections if equally distinct, verified content exists.
- **55–59s: color highlights selection.** A large dark pricing zone contains a selected option whose detail panel changes to green. Selection explains the color change. Langia can translate that state with Mist + restrained Signal Blue rather than copying surface saturation.
- **60–66s: quiet FAQ.** Small centered reading width surrounded by white space, with restrained topic selection and expanding answers. The scene provides rest after richer compositions.
- **66.5–68.5s: short resources scene.** Two visual articles and a quiet index action. No full-viewport lock is needed to communicate a list of resources.
- **69–73.5s: final photo converts.** Large photographic field, light translucent form, one concrete booking action. The form is visually subordinate to the scene but immediately understandable.
- **75–81s: the wordmark is the ending.** A pale footer surface supports useful small navigation and one oversized wordmark. Langia’s white/Mist ending and giant LANGIA can use this hierarchy directly.
