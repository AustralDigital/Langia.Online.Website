## Measured layout and behavior

- **Global frames:** full-bleed feature surfaces use about 20 px outer inset (`x=20..1900`) with roughly 35-42 px radii. Main editorial content repeatedly uses `x=220..1700`, about **1480 px / 77.2vw**.
- **Persistent nav:** approximately `x=30, y=28, w=593, h=60`, 30 px pill radius. It is fixed for the entire page and sits above every wipe/surface. The separate hero “Book a call” pill is not fixed; it leaves with hero foreground.
- **Hero:** photo is about `1880 x 907` at rest. Headline starts near `x=80, y=135`, about 80 px type with tight line-height. The case-study card is roughly `450 x 220` at the lower right. Foreground translates substantially faster than actual scroll.
- **Hero image transition:** inset image becomes full bleed while scaling; blur is visually around a strong 10-15 px class blur, accompanied by a dark overlay. The blur onset is eased in time after crossing its scroll threshold; the scrollbar barely moves during the strongest 8.2-8.7 s change.
- **Transformation cards:** settled cards are about `500 x 650`, 26vw x 69vh, radius about 35 px; two-card row is about 1020 px wide. The scene uses a pinned/centered composition over roughly 3 viewport-heights of scroll.
- **Services/programs:** every card is approximately `1480 x 690` (77vw x 73vh), top pinned near 122 px, with near-equal text and image halves and an 8-12 px internal gutter. The reference does **not** alternate text/image sides; all shown cards are text-left, image-right. Each successor overlays the prior card rather than merely following it with a gap.
- **Process expanding row:** about `1480 x 400`, radius about 30 px, gaps around 10 px. Four panels distribute approximately **720 px active + 240 px each inactive** (near 3:1). Content opacity and background color animate together with width.
- **Testimonial hero:** about `1480 x 840`, radius about 32 px. Display title is around 110 px. The glass quote card is about `380 x 380`.
- **Testimonial expanding row:** about 1480 x 335. With three items, active is roughly 730 px and inactive roughly 360 px each (near 2:1). This is hover-driven and changes in 0.2-0.3 s.
- **Type scale at this viewport:** main display headings are generally 80-112 px; major card headings 30-48 px; body copy 16-20 px. Large empty vertical intervals of 150-250 px are intentional.
- **Final CTA/footer:** CTA repeats the 20 px inset/35 px radius language. Footer begins as a circular color wipe, not a rectangular section scroll. The cream footer card animates horizontally as it rises; its final outer margin is about 24 px. The final wordmark is several hundred pixels tall and deliberately cropped at the viewport bottom.
- **Layer order to preserve:** fixed nav; incoming takeover/card; current content; background/photo. In service stacks, later cards are above earlier cards. In the finale, green circular wipe is above the CTA, cream footer above the wipe, fixed nav above all.

## Important recording caveats

- 34.0-37.0 s is a deliberate **upward scroll** from about 15.03 vh back to 13.74 vh to revisit the process panels. Do not model it as a second copy of the section.
- 37.0-40.0 s, 45-47 s, 50-51.5 s, 57-59 s, 60-66 s, and parts of 67-68.5 s include pointer-driven interactions while scroll is stationary or nearly stationary.
- The circular up/down control at mid-right and the Polar/“Made in Framer” overlays at bottom-right are third-party/demo chrome visible in the recording. They are not part of the target page structure.
